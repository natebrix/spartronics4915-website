Here are the competition dates for 2015 FIRST FRC "Recycle Rush" events that Spartronics Team 4915 will compete in. Some team members will be required to travel the day before to set up the Pit, ready Robot GAEA:

![IMG_0578-225x300](/images/posts/district-events-save-the-dates/IMG_0578-225x3001.jpg)

**Glacier Peak - Glacier Peak High, Snohomish, WA**  
_March 7-8th (Sat & Sun)_

**Shorewood - Shorewood High | Shoreline, WA**  
_March 21- 22nd (Sat & Sun)_

**PNW Championship - Cheney, WA**  
_April 2-4 (Thurs-Sat)_

If we rank high enough, we may qualify for the World Championships at the Edward Jones Dome in St. Louis:

**World Championships - St. Louis, Mo**.  
_Apr 23-25 (Thurs-Sat)_
