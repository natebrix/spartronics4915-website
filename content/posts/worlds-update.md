Spartronics has been competing in quarterfinals in the Carver division over the past two days. After all the quarterfinals matches on Friday, we stand in 28th place in our division. Our qualification average is 131.30 points. In addition, in our 5th match on Friday, our alliance scored an incredible 265 points, our season record! Alliance selection begins tomorrow and we all wish everyone in St. Louis good luck and congratulate them on the stellar job they have already done.
