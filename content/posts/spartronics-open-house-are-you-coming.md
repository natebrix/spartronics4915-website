#### JOIN US AT

## Bainbridge High School WEDNESDAY May 4th, 5-7 pm **Robotics Open House**

![team photo](/images/posts/spartronics-open-house-are-you-coming/team-photo.jpg)

Please join us on **Wednesday, May 4th** for our Robotics Open House, showcasing Robotics on Bainbridge Island. We want teachers, students of all ages, families, friends, sponsors, parents, and community members to come to see STEAM/STEM - and our robots - in action!

Our two-month robot build season for Spartronics Team 4915 -- which is entailed the arduous effort of some 40 students and 10 professional mentors, and Coaches Enrique Chee and Austin Smith -- culminated in our robot HERMES making it to the District Championship!

![HERMES in action](/images/posts/spartronics-open-house-are-you-coming/hermes-in-action.jpg)

Join us and see HERMES in action and learn more about what our team does!

We'll share what we've been doing while having the “hardest fun we’ll ever have.”

You’ll meet our programming, mechanical, electrical/pneumatic, and business and marketing sub-teams at this student-led event.

You'll also have the opportunity to learn about FIRST, which offers many post-high-school opportunities to our students.

And most importantly, we would love to chat with you and answer any questions you might have about our team!

We hope to see you there!
