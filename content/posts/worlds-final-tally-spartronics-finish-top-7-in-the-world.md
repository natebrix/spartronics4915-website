Spartronics Team 4915 had the 206th highest average score among the 608 of the world's best robots that competed at the FIRST FRC World Championships in St. Louis. That places Spartronics in the Top 7% of the more than 3,000 high school teams from around the globe that competed in this year's "Recycle Rush" challenge.

Highlight of the competition was the 265 score the team made as part of a winning alliance match. It was the second highest score of their Carver Division, with the top score 268!

Spartronics met with teams from China, Taiwan, The Netherlands, Australia, London and Mexico to name a few.
