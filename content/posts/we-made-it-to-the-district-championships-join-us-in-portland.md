We have qualified for 2016 FIRST Robotics District Championship! ARES is ready to help take the tower and breach the defenses! We have marched to the battlefield and helped our Alliances succeed and now we go on to join the best of the best from Washington, Oregon and Alaska at the Veterans Memorial Coliseum in Portland! Join us -- the matches are free to everyone. No LIVESTREAM has been announced yet - but check back here or watch our social media channels

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-iNFaJNdlr6c/AAAAAAAAAAI/AAAAAAAAABU/rigR9Wc020I/s0-c-k-no-ns/photo.jpg)

**WHERE**

The event will be held at the Veterans Memorial Coliseum, 300 North Winning Way, Portland, OR 97227, where we will compete against the top 64 teams in the PNW District. This is a FREE public event so head on down and show your Spartronics pride!

**EAT**

For those who attend the event we have a food delivery service in Portland called Caviar. It will deliver food from the most exclusive and best restaurants in Portland. Visit https://www.trycaviar.com/portland for more information. Note the venue also will have food services and no outside food can be brought inside, but there are areas to eat outside the venue and the weather is supposed to be clear.

**STAY**

The team will be at the Days Inn Portland, north of downtown at 9930 N Whitaker Rd, Portland, OR 97217. There is Best Western across the street and other accommodations nearby.
