This is Ace Hardware’s third year as a Gold Sponsor for Spartronics Team 4915. We love our sponsors, as they help us build futures within the team. Thanks so much Ace!

_Pictured: Steve Mikami, owner of Ace Hardware, presenting Mentor Bill Davis with the $1000 sponsorship check._

It’s not too late to become a Sponsor for Spartronics Team 4915. Sponsor logos go everywhere - on our shirts, robot, and our website. The deadline is now December 15th.

Boeing is back as a Major Sponsor along with Clare and Anthony Liguori.

Westside Pizza returns as a Silver Sponsor along with Destination Imagination, Mr. Shutt, and Town and Country Markets.

For sponsorship information please contact [spartronics4915@gmail.com](mailto:spartronics4915@gmail.com).
