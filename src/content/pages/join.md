We want *you* to join us! Pick one of our five subteams and learn skills you can use in the real world. You will learn how to use industry-grade machines, programs, platforms and techniques, and gain business and marketing experience too. You will also gain leadership experience, lifelong friendships and the thrill of FRC competitions.

There is no requirement to have any previous experience in STEAM or robotics. We will teach you everything you need throughout the year as we build our new robot. All we ask is a can-do attitude, an open mind, and that you attend an island high school or something equivalent.

## When and where we meet

- **Before Kickoff:** Wednesdays, 6–8 PM. Kickoff is in early January, when the game is unveiled and build season starts.
- **After Kickoff:** Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, 4–8 PM.
- **Where:** BHS 100 Building, Room 103.

We know it can be hard to make every meeting, so there's no attendance requirement. Even coming to part of a meeting makes a big difference! Check the [calendar](/calendar/) for upcoming dates, and email [spartronics4915@gmail.com](mailto:spartronics4915@gmail.com) with any questions.

## About us

Spartronics began its first season in 2013, founded by a physics teacher and a business mentor. Almost every season since, Spartronics' robots have won awards, placed highly, or won competitions outright. See [every award the team has won](/awards/), and meet our robots in [Our History](/history/).
