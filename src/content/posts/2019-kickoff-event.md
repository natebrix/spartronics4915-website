We gathered before 7:00 am on Saturday, January 5th for the live video broadcast on the FIRST Twitch channel, joining 3,700 FRC teams around the globe, including 436 Rookie teams!

We also sent two student members - team Co-Captain and Marketing lead Chris Mentzer and freshman Mechanical subteam member Mitchell Teresi - and Mentor Kevin Hawkins to the Mountlake Terrace kickoff hosted by FRC Team 1778 Chill Out. Mountlake Terrace was one of 146 official FIRST Kickoff events worldwide and where our teammates picked up our Kit of Parts to help us build our 2019 robot for the new game.
