Join us March 2nd for the 3rd open house! Last year our 2nd annual open house garnered over 500 attendees, and we hope to bring in even more this year. Open House is scheduled for Wednesday, March 2nd from 6 – 8 pm, with all our local Robotics teams — students from grades 4th through 12th including FIRST Robotics FLL, FTC and FRC teams participating! This event is at Bainbridge Island Highschool in the 300 building. This will be the first time our new robot for this year’s STRONGHOLD game will be unveiled, as well as allow the public to see our design process and learn about the subteams.
