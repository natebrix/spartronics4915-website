**The Final Senior Spotlight!** Our final senior of the year is Joyce Michalczuk, one of our Mechanics captains! They have been with the team for four years, their entire time at Bainbridge High School. Joyce has played a large part in constructing our last four robots and has also helped teach new members how to use various machines in the shop. They are widely considered a master teacher among new members. Joyce is honest and hardworking at competitions where they have deep insight into strategy, maintenance and problem-solving. Joyce's dream career is in Civil Engineering, a field where all of these traits will assist them.We will see you next year with new seniors's profiles!
