Newsletter by Carly Hart, Maxwell Richards, and Henry Tuohy.

Spartronics has started off strong this year with the introduction of many new members to our team!

Due to the influx of students new to the team, we have spent much of the past few weeks training new teammates in safety and proper use of equipment.

The team has also been fulfilling its usual annual tasks of taking inventory of supplies, getting new members familiarized with FIRST, and organizing our workspaces.

A few notable accomplishments have been made by the team already, including the completion of a working swerve drive chassis prototype. Additionally, the team has seen its Marketing subteam grow substantially, even receiving a new mentor at the start of the year.
