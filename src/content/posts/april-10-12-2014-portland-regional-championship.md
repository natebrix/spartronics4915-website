Due to our success at our previous two events, we qualified for the District Championship in Oregon! Here we were tested against the top 62 teams between Oregon and Washington; only the 24 highest ranked teams qualify for the World Championships in St. Louis, MO. We competed well, landing in 13th place of 63 teams, and won the District Championship-level Rookie Inspiration Award, which put us firmly in the top 24! We're going to St. Louis in our first year!
